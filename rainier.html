Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
318 AM PDT Fri Oct 23 2026

High pressure aloft will provide another day of dry conditions for Western Washington on Friday. An area of low pressure moving southward from the British Columbia coast will bring some rain or higher elevation snow to the National Park later Saturday into the early portion of next week. Drier conditions are expected to return by the middle of next week as the low pressure system moves eastward.
