Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
301 AM PDT Mon Aug 24 2026

High pressure building into the Pacific Northwest will bring warm and dry conditions to Mount Rainier National Park today and Tuesday. Easterly winds in the low levels will bring smoke from nearby wildfires to the park at times through Tuesday, before an improvement in conditions is expected on Wednesday. A weather system moving into the region midweek will bring cooler temperatures, chances of precipitation, and increased cloud cover the second half of the week.
