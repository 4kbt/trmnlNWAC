Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
302 PM PDT Fri Aug 14 2026

A trough over the area today will bring chances of thunderstorms to Mount Rainier National Park through this evening. Any thunderstorms that do develop may produce abundant lightning and gusty, erratic outflow winds. Areas of patchy smoke will also be prevalent across the park. Additional rounds of thunderstorms will be possible across the park on Saturday, but will gradually wane throughout the weekend as high pressure builds into the area from the southeast. Another weather system will move into the region early next week.
