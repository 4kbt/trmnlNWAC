Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
225 PM PDT Mon Oct 12 2026

Dry conditions are expected to continue across Mount Rainier National Park over the next few days. High pressure building into the region midweek will bring above normal temperatures to the park on Thursday and Friday. A weak system will then move into the park Friday night into Saturday for increasing rain and snow chances heading into the weekend. A stronger system arriving Sunday into Monday will likely bring stronger winds, falling snow levels, and increasing chances of precipitation across the park into next week.
