Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
411 PM PDT Mon Aug 3 2026

Dry and warmer conditions as an upper level ridge offshore moves inland Wednesday and Thursday. A thermally induced trough will develop tonight and remain over the region through Wednesday, resulting in northeasterly winds advecting smoke to Western Washington. The ridge will push eastward late Thursday. Smokey conditions should improve by the end of the week with increased onshore flow. Cooler temperatures favored for the weekend.
