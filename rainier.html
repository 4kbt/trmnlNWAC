Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
247 PM PDT Sun Oct 4 2026

Upper level ridging over western Washington will flatten out as a system passes to the north with near zonal flow aloft expected by Monday. This will keep temperatures near seasonable normals as well as return onshore flow to the area. The pattern of morning clouds giving way to afternoon sun will persist at least through mid-week. An approaching frontal system for the end of the week could bring the next chances for precipitation, however some uncertainty exists.
