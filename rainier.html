Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
218 AM PST Sun Nov 1 2026

Dry conditions will continue across Mount Rainier National Park into Monday with high pressure over the region. Winds will become strong near the summit of the mountain on Monday night and some light precipitation will be possible at the park late Monday into Tuesday. A more unsettled weather pattern the second half of the week will bring more notable chances of precipitation to the park as a series of systems moves across the Pacific Northwest.
