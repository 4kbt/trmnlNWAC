Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
222 PM PDT Mon Sep 28 2026

A weather system moving across western Washington Monday night into Tuesday will bring strong winds, rain, and snow to Mount Rainier National Park. Drier conditions are expected at the park on Wednesday, before slight chances of rain and snow return Thursday into Friday. Overall drier and sunnier conditions are expected to return to the park by the weekend as high pressure builds back into the region.
