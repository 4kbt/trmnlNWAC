Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
254 AM PDT Tue Aug 25 2026

High pressure will continue to bring warm and dry conditions to Mount Rainier National Park today. Patchy smoke will also be prevalent throughout the day, before an improvement in conditions is expected on Wednesday. A pattern change will bring cooler conditions to the park on Wednesday and through the remainder of the week. Precipitation chances increase on Thursday and will persist at times through the weekend.
