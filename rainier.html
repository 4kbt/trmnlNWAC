Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
215 PM PDT Wed Aug 12 2026

Showers and thunderstorms will continue over the mountains over the next several days under the influence of an upper level trough. A Fire Weather Watch remains in effect for Thursday due to thunderstorms that could bring abundant lightning and gusty outflow winds. Southwest flow aloft will develop over the weekend, bringing some warming and continued chances for high elevation thunderstorms ahead of another weak trough at the beginning of next week that will likely bring slightly cooler and unsettled conditions.
