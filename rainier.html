Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
318 AM PDT Mon Aug 3 2026

An upper level ridge will build offshore Monday and Monday night then move inland Tuesday and Wednesday. Thermally induced surface trough developing over the area Monday night will remain in place through at least Wednesday night creating northerly flow in the lower levels. This will pull some smoke over the park. The ridge will shift eastward later Thursday into Friday. Increasing low level onshore flow at the end of the week with cooler temperatures and less smoke.
