Mount Rainier Recreational Forecast
National Weather Service Seattle WA
300 AM PST Wed Nov 11 2026

A warm front has introduced a very mild air mass to Western Washington. A cold front will stall offshore into Thursday with mainly dry conditions for the National Park. A series of frontal systems will bring a return of widespread rain and high elevation snow Friday into the weekend. Snow levels are expected to drop to below Paradise by the early portion of next week.
